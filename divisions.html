Division comparison

One row per division, plus Unassigned, for the dates you choose. Asset counts, fuel, and running cost add up to the account total. Rates are per asset, per kilometre, or per operating hour, so a smaller division is compared on the same basis as a larger one.

Lowest and highest mark the ends of each rate in this period. They compare the divisions with each other. A higher or lower figure does not mean the data is wrong. Select a division to open the analyst page for that set of assets.
